        Worth a closer look

        5 signs a tree may fall on your house

        If you notice these warning signs, it may be time to call a professional.

        1. 01 Leaning toward a structure

          A tree that’s started to lean toward the house, garage, or a driveway is a warning, not just a quirk of the yard.

        2. 02 Dead limbs falling

          Branches dropping in calm weather often mean there’s more dead wood waiting above.

        3. 03 Hollow or rotting trunk

          Cavities, fungus, or soft, decaying wood can weaken the tree from the inside.

        4. 04 Roots lifting out of the ground

          Heaving soil or exposed, lifting roots can mean the tree is losing its hold.

        5. 05 Cracks running up the trunk

          Vertical splits in the trunk are a structural red flag, especially after storms.
